### Project Description

Sanchari-Link is a web platform that connects Malayalis living abroad with trusted local youth in Kerala to complete small real‑world tasks on their behalf. Users can post tasks easily and receive local assistance without depending only on relatives or multiple phone calls.

### The Problem statement

Many Malayalis living outside Kerala struggle to manage small local tasks such as document collection, bill payments, visits, or verifications. Finding reliable people locally is difficult, time‑consuming, and often unsafe.

### The Solution

Sanchari-Link provides a simple digital platform where users can post tasks and connect with verified local youth who can complete them. The platform reduces communication effort, builds trust, and enables faster local support.
